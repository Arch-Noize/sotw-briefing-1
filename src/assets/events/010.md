The atmosphere at the College remains tense and focused. Students and faculty alike keep a weather eye on militaristic posturing coming from the Baronic Council and Ignoble House, and conversations about the future inevitably turn toward the prospect of deployment. The shift in attitude is most significant during classes, as a new, cold seriousness underscores each lesson. Even with Iphianassa's continued training and rewards (granting them **Effective Coolants** in the process), the group of kuirassers could feel like there could be a bigger conflict developing under their nose.

To make matters somewhat worse, a juicy rumor of **Count Argo** and **Quinn** being in some sort of relationship reached her friends thanks to the Primors. Almost as if to verify these rumors, Yarin managed to find her being cornered by a very flirtatious Argo with her *clearly* looking uncomfortable. The hacker from Tilimsan managed to eavesdrop on them until Argo left, 'expecting to hear soon from Quinn and that she would have time for a second time'. Confronting her shortly after, Yarin learns that the shy and small squire is the last of her house and that she too was duty bound, much like Leman. She saw an opportunity with Argo, but clearly looks ashamed and uncomfortable for having made that 'connection' once classes were postponed a week ago. As much as Yarin tried to comfort her about 'not needing to meet expectations', his words fell flat, with Quinn thanking him for trying but staying firm that this is not something she can't escape.

`Later, on another class...`

As everyone was listening to the strategies and data analysis from Lord Hesketh, a College functionary wordlessly delivers a message bearing the distinctive seal of the **Xenoglossary** to the kuirassers, reading as follows: 
> *The hour draws near, as does the blade. Be not in your beds when they strike for your heart. Your savior moves worlds for you – gird yourselves and do not tarry at their call.*
> — RV 21-98-37

As it bared the seal of the Augur, the students tried to decipher the prophetic message delivered to them. All of them—in their own way—managed to piece together this was a warning of an attack and that they must leave College grounds *immediately*, as well as something awaiting them in a specific gate near The Stables. As the kuirassers all rush out of the class and quickly gather to make quick plans on their way to the gate, they all receive a flurry of notifications reaches their devices. 

First comes an order from the dean to immediately report to her office. Second, a message from one of their friends with a message saying something along the lines of, “this can’t possibly be real, can it?”, along with an attached breaking news story. **Including Kasha, who simply wrote "I want to trust you" before blocking his contact.** It rapidly becomes clear that somebody has released a large body of doctored evidence onto the Karrakis-local omninet, framing the group for being involved in the attack on the Hounds Teeth Range. The accusations involve a series of absolutely wild misrepresentations regarding them and their actions (and include several outright fabrications), but it appears that security forces are acting on them out of an abundance of caution. They can find a wide spread of false information released with suspiciously coordinated timing—a clear smear campaign—one seemingly spearheaded by the comments *from Lord Praya in an interview with a local news outlet*.

Rushing and arriving at the Stables, they are met by **Hale Saremin**, assistant chief of the construction crew and the person primarily responsible for landscaping logistics. Hale has prepared a transport hauler plastered with distinctive warning signs – “CAUTION: HAZARDOUS CONTENTS” and “NANITES IN TRANSIT”. Fortunately, the hauler is empty. Hale tells the students they had instructions from Augur Kahin to load up their mechs (which barely fit), and transport them to a specific location in the city, far away from the College. Tensions were high, distrust grew, and panic was settling in... but if it was organized by the Augur, then they will trust in the transport.

The kuirassers were quickly and silently taken out of the College, giving them barely a moment of respite to repair their mechs with limited tools and investigate the allegations further. All feeling like they were meat for the grinder.
