The students prepare for their first combat exercise since returning to the College. Rather than the hypothetical scenarios they have become used to, closer to duels than real combat, Iphianassa’s exercises now focus on what the College refers to as “adversary chassis” – with the clear subtext that the students are now training for specific combat scenarios against specific enemies to the KTB... With their latest aims being the **Ungratefuls**.

These training scenarios take place on a rotating basis, with students designated as “adversaries” piloting a mix of their own mechs and common Ungratefuls designs. The combat's briefing adds that weapon safety guidelines have been reviewed and consequently removed, allowing the students to fight more aggressively, whilst also testing their trust with one another. To offset the increased damage, Iphianassa has declared that the winning side will be rewarded with *additional print credits.*

There were four "objectives". Four zones but only one held the "explosive device" setup by the Ungratefuls. The OpFor team—which included their rowdy acquaintance **Rawan and Lord Tomas**—had to distract and protect the **True Zone**, while the kuirassers had to find which of the four was the True Zone and control it by the end of the simulation.

What's confusing however, is throughout the entire briefing, as well as check ups on the system... Yarin couldn't get I.K.O.L to come up or even answer his texts. Nothing bad should've happened, otherwise there would be signs, but it was a first to have the brotherly NHP ghost around... That was until the kuirassers arrived to the combat grounds. Yarin took a single step with his mech—Fiend—before it literally broke into hundreds of pieces as a cocoon of a suit wrapped around Yarin. I.K.O.L finally came alive in everyone's feed with a loud and excited "S̶U̸R̷P̸R̷I̶S̶E̷~̴!", revealing **Vali**, his new frame. Yarin had plenty of questions but he'd have to save them for after the combat. As well as have some answers in the middle of it.

Despite having new technologies, strategies, and weaponry at their hands, the students shan't disappoint Iphianassa:
- There was a widespread moment of confusion and uncertainty to recognize the true objective, but the students moved forward and together as a team.
- **Yarin** tested the new 'tricks' that I.K.O.L had ready for him with **Vali**. Including... concerning and dangerous routes of access to even block out life support systems.
- **CALIGRAND** and **Leman** took a slow start, but eventually pushed right unto enemy lines to start ripping and tearing all on their way, with **CASKET** and **Fenrir** respectively.
- And lastly, **Camille** and **Quinn** took care of protecting their allies... Though through wildly different strategies. The Lady using dazzling technology to force targeting systems to walk towards her like moths to flame with **Center of the Universe**; while the Squire used her large size to protect the very very tiny Yarin with an upgraded **Honesty**.

After the time ran out, the non-adversary kuirassers managed to win the simulation. All students were called to Iphianassa’s office after the combat. She leads the group in a dissection of the fight and their tactics, criticizing them harshly but constructively of how they could've done better, and summarize what they learned from the combat. After the "feedback" session on their debriefing, Iphianassa rewards them with extra print credits for their use. (**`Extra Repairs reserve`**)

After being dismissed, the kuirassers are able to vent and talk about how intense the college has become with the prospects of war. Rawan commenting on how it is “bullshit” that the College is “training us to suppress people defending their homes”. Everyone is uneasy and uncomfortable, specially Yarin, but alas... they can't sit around to complain. They must prepare, whether they like it or not.
