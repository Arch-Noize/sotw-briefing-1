Rushed out of the college, the students barely get moments to recollect themselves. Quinn and Yarin took their time to study the accusations, while the others spent time to do some quick repairs. Slowly, they started registering the sounds of a city filled with life and activity, as they thread their way through an industrial district to a warehouse at its heart. Judging by the fading paintwork and the dust that kicks up as the kuirassers reach the entrance, this building has not been in regular use for some time. Inside, they are confronted by the eerie sight of rows of unfinished subaltern bodies stacked next to racks of robotic heads that stare into space with lifeless eyes. The creepy scene broken by the sight of the Xenogloss Augur garbed almost entirely in the concealing robes of his order.

Kahin greets the group and hurries them inside along with their mechs, muttering something about not having time. He quickly apologizes for not explaining his warning further and dismisses his bodyguard, **Soto**, just as quickly after. He told the students to steel themselves of the danger to come, and that time was of the essence... Or at least he was saying that before a mech-sized metal javelin plunges through the side of the warehouse, barely missing the students, but striking the augur in the torso, and impaling him against a nearby wall.

The assailant that attacked the Kahin was... **none other than Quinn**, who had slipped out of sight without anyone questioning or batting an eye. Retrofitted with high end invisibility technology, Quinn briefly presents herself and apologizes.
> "Hello, guys. First I want to say that... this... isn't personal. I'm just doing my job."
> 
> "I truly _am_ sorry. For what it's worth... I appreciated the time we spent together."

Weapons fire and chaos breaks out! The escapees are under attack by unknown assailants! These are assassins from the House of Sand (**the infamous Sand-men**), and their mission here is fairly unambiguous – they are trying to kill them. There's a limited amount of time to hold out before help arrives, or at the very least make enough noise so someone realizes. Quick hornet-like mechs, drone commanders, a group of robotic scouts, and **Count Argo** himself surrounded them. For now, the students had but one objective: ***Survive.***

As much as they tried to stick together, CALIGRAND, Yarin, and Leman took some **heavy** damage as the fight broke off from Quinn's mech. Her chasis seemed *tailored* to defend herself from their strengths and exploit their weaknesses. High-end invisibility technology on top of stealth and jamming protocols, deadly accurate weaponry and systems, heavily encrypted firewalls... Afterwards, they all tried to defend the best they could, with Yarin sticking to the back of the Center of the Universe. Leman, as valiant in spirit as he may've been was caught and punished for going all out, literally. Pinned and ambushed as many enemies surrounded him. Lastly, CALIGRAND and Camille diverted their focus almost entirely on Count Argo's mech, ignoring all other enemies.

Alas, the time to strike and their window of opportunity had escaped the Sand-men's fingers. The rest of the forces started leaving immediately once alarms finally rung, just as Count Argo's mech is destroyed. It took plenty of effort just to survive the attack... it took a ***miracle*** that they only managed to catch and stop their arrogant 'bully'.

Clearly crippled by the attack, if not outright brittle, the students have minutes before security forces arrive at the scene as Soto, weirdly covered in blood, instructed them to quickly leave the scene with the hauler.
